# Q2 2026 earnings referee — evidence file

The Scissors (2026-07-10) named Q2 earnings Jul 22–Aug 5 as the referee for its
three signal changes (FCX BUY→HOLD, GEV BUY→HOLD, MOD HOLD→BUY) and its central
claim: the market has stopped trading current results and started trading the
lens's second acts (2027-28 overbuild risk, tariff-propped copper, moat
compression). Collect evidence per report here; write the scored piece after
MHI on Aug 4.

Schedule: VRT Jul 29 pre-market ✓ · MOD Jul 29 after close (call Jul 30) ·
PWR Jul 30 · ETN, NVT Jul 31 · MHI Aug 4.

## VRT — reported 2026-07-29 pre-market (data gathered same day)

**Business half (The Heat Wall thesis: BUY, entry $295):**
- Revenue $3,274M +24% YoY (organic +18%, acquisitions +5%, FX +1%) — but ~3%
  BELOW consensus $3.38B
- Adjusted diluted EPS $1.52 +60% YoY, beat consensus $1.42
- Adjusted operating margin 22.6%, +410 bp YoY
- Operating cash flow $1,100M +241%; adjusted FCF $925M +234%; $5.6B liquidity,
  net cash
- FY2026 guidance RAISED: sales $13.8–14.2B (organic +30–32%), adj EPS
  $6.65–6.75 (+58–61%), FCF $2.4–2.6B, margin 23.3–24.3%
- Q3 guide: organic +34–36% — growth ACCELERATING, not decelerating
- No backlog/book-to-bill in the release (watch the call transcript); capex ~4%
  of revenue, "continual capacity expansions underway"

**Equity half:**
- Price path: 07-23 $304 → 07-27 $287.60 → 07-28 ~$269 (-6.3% "Tuesday") →
  07-29 $227.40 intraday (-15%), roughly -25% in four sessions
- vs entry $295: ~-23%. The book's largest drawdown.
- The sale happened on a RAISED-guidance, accelerating-growth quarter, keyed to
  a 3% revenue miss vs sell-side models.

**Day-one referee reading:** the scissors dynamic CONFIRMED in its strongest
form yet — fundamentals accelerated while the equity was repriced down a
quarter. What Q2 does NOT settle: whether this is multiple-compression toward
fair (The Scissors' warning: elevated multiples pulled forward years of
execution) or an overshoot creating entry. The distinction belongs to the
final piece, not day one. Note the asymmetry vs GEV: VRT's punishment came
WITH a guidance raise, which is more purely a multiple event than GEV's
Barclays-call selloff.

**Not yet recorded**: whether the revenue miss was timing (deployment schedules
push revenue right — the bull explanation) or demand (the bear one). Q3 guide
of +34–36% organic argues timing. Check the call transcript summary before the
final piece asserts either.

## MOD — reported after close 2026-07-29 (data gathered 07-30 morning)

**Business half (The Scissors upgraded HOLD→BUY 07-10 on the LTA/moat evidence):**
- Revenue $874.1M +28% YoY — below the $895.5M consensus (~2.4% miss)
- Adjusted diluted EPS $1.53, above the $1.38 consensus
- Adjusted EBITDA $106.5M
- **Data Centers: $348.6M, +90% YoY** (vs $183.7M) on North America hyperscale —
  the demand half of the upgrade thesis confirmed emphatically
- **Data Centers gross margin 20.2%, DOWN 960 bp YoY** — capacity-expansion
  expenses + production inefficiencies from supply-chain constraints + material
  costs + warranty. This is the flagged component-shortage risk materializing.
- FY27 guidance REAFFIRMED (sales +20–35%, adj EBITDA $650–680M) — not raised

**Equity half:**
- 07-27 $230.40 → 07-28 $208.06 (-9.7%) → 07-29 $178.04 (-14.4%), i.e. -23% in
  two sessions, and almost all of it BEFORE its own release (07-29's drop was
  sympathy with VRT's print). After-hours reaction to its own numbers not yet
  captured; check 07-30 close.

**Day-one referee reading:** splits the upgrade thesis cleanly in two. Volume:
confirmed (+90%). Near-term economics: dinged (960 bp segment gross-margin
compression). The bull frame is that expansion costs are the construction bill
for the contracted moat (the $4B LTA has to be built before it pays); the bear
frame is that supply-chain inefficiency during the buildout is exactly when a
"moat under construction" behaves like a cost center. Guidance reaffirmed says
management sees the margin dip as temporary. The referee's real verdict needs
the FY27 margin trajectory, not one buildout quarter.

**Sector observation for the final piece:** VRT's 3% revenue miss repriced the
entire complex (MOD -23%, PWR -9.5% pre-own-print). The market is treating one
company's deployment-timing wobble as thesis-level evidence against the group —
which is the scissors dynamic operating at sector granularity, and worth its
own paragraph.
